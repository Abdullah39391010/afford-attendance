1. What the system does

A complete attendance and payroll system for about 70 staff at Afford Super Store, in English and Urdu, that costs nothing to run.

  • Employees check in and out from their own phone, but only when they are inside the store's GPS circle.
  • Admin and managers see a live board of who is in, late, on break, absent or on leave. They approve requests and run the monthly salary statements.
  • Salary is worked out automatically from attendance. That includes late cuts, early-leave cuts, late return from break, absences, unpaid leave, advances and security deposit.
  • Staff without a phone use a shared kiosk phone at the counter, or a manager checks them in.
  • The owner approves money and salary changes made by managers, locks paid months, and gets a nightly summary and alerts on the phone.
  • Staff get reminders before their shift and when they forget to check out, and must accept the store rules in the app.
  • Data is backed up every day to separate cloud storage (30 days kept) and to the owner's phone.
  • Every admin action is written to an audit log.

2. How it fits together

All data lives in one place, the store's database on Cloudflare. The admin app, employee phones and the kiosk all read from and write to the same database through the Worker. A copy is saved every day in a separate backup vault in the same Cloudflare account. Alerts to phones go through Google Firebase and carry only short text. Nothing else leaves your account.

4. Employee app

  • First login: employee code (or phone number) and PIN given by admin. The phone is then locked to that employee. No one else can log in on it, and the employee can't log in on another phone until the owner resets it. Both the server and the app enforce this.
  • Next time: fingerprint or face unlock.
  • First open after an update of the rules: the store rules are shown and the employee must tick "I agree". The date is saved.
  • Big Check In / Check Out buttons. Check-in works only inside the store circle. Check-out asks "Are you sure?" first. Delivery boys, purchasers and field staff can check out from anywhere.
  • Checking in again the same day needs approval. After check-out, the employee taps "Ask to check in again" (only from inside the store). Once the admin or a manager approves, they can check in once, and the time away is counted as late.
  • Reminders: an alert 15 minutes before the shift if not checked in, and "Forgot to check out?" 30 minutes after the shift ends.
  • Today card: in and out time, hours worked, late minutes and break status.
  • Break: ask for a break with minutes, then tap Resume when back. Coming back late is counted and cut.
  • Requests: forgot check-in or check-out, past day, leave, advance, "checked out by mistake" and out-of-town duty.
  • Calendar with coloured days and a legend, a leave balance line, the salary statement (as allowed by admin, with overtime and "salary paid" shown) and a security deposit card.
  • English or Urdu, chosen at login. Urdu screens are right-to-left. The layout uses large, clear buttons for non-technical staff.
  • Update popup showing the new version number and what's new. More › "Check for update" checks at once.
  • iPhone: the apps are Android only. An iPhone user checks in on the kiosk or through a co-worker's helper phone.
Salary visibility (set per employee): "Always" shows the full monthly statement. "Last 7 days" shows only the last 7 days of attendance and cuts, with no monthly total.

5. Staff without a phone (Kiosk)

Some staff, such as guards and cleaners, don't have a smartphone. There are two ways to handle them, and both can be used.

A. Shared kiosk phone

  • One phone stays at the counter in Kiosk mode.
  • The employee taps their photo or name and types their own PIN.
  • The front camera takes a photo, kept for 90 days, so admin can check it was really them.
  • It works only inside the store circle.

B. Manager checks them in

  • From the live board, tap the employee and choose Check in or Check out.
  • A reason is required, for example "No phone".
  • It is written to the audit log.

A fingerprint sensor can't be used on a shared phone, because Android only checks the phone owner's fingerprint and can't tell 70 people apart. That's why the kiosk uses PIN plus photo.

6. Out-of-town staff (Field staff)

  • Tick Field staff on the employee, for example purchasers who travel. They can check in and out from anywhere.
  • The GPS point of every punch is saved, so admin can see how far from the store they were and open it on a map.
  • For a full day away, the employee sends an Out-of-town duty request, or admin marks the day as duty. It counts as present, with no late or early cuts.

7. Owner web page

Open afford-attendance.abdullah6763939.workers.dev/owner/ on any computer. It is separate from the admin logins: it asks only for the owner page code. After the code it shows everything the owner sees: board, requests, staff, payroll, reports, roster and backups.

The page is not mentioned anywhere in the admin app, so managers and family who use the app don't see it. The code is kept on GitHub as the secret OWNER_PAGE_CODE (Settings › Secrets and variables › Actions) and reaches the server with the next deploy. To change it, edit that secret and run the deploy again; every open owner page is then logged out. After 5 wrong codes the page is locked for 5 minutes, then longer each time. A login lasts 12 hours. Phone-only parts are hidden on the web page.

9. Payroll formulas and worked example

daily rate = monthly salary ÷ days basis (26, 30 or days in month) hourly rate = daily rate ÷ shift hours per-minute = hourly rate ÷ 60 late cut = late minutes × per-minute early cut = early-leave minutes × per-minute break cut = late-return minutes × per-minute absent cut = absent days × daily rate unpaid leave = unpaid leave days × daily rate before joining = working days before joining date × daily rate after leaving = days after the last working day × daily rate time away = minutes between a check-out and the next check-in (same day) → added to late minutes overtime pay = minutes after shift end × per-minute × overtime rate (when switched on) net pay = salary − all cuts − advances − deposit cut + bonus + overtime pay

Weekly offs, holidays, paid leave and out-of-town duty are paid. The statement for the current month shows the figures so far.

The security deposit balance is shown on the statement. It is deducted from pay only when admin records a "deposit cut".

12. Salary day: payslips, payment record, settlement

  1. Open Payroll, pick last month and check the totals.
  2. Open each employee, check the payslip, then Share PDF to send it on WhatsApp, or Print.
  3. Pay the salary and tap Mark salary as paid (cash, bank or other, with a note such as a cheque or JazzCash number). The employee gets an alert. The Payroll list shows ✓ Paid and how many are still unpaid.
  4. When everyone is paid, tap Salary paid? Lock this month.

When someone leaves

  1. Open the employee and tap Final settlement. Pick the last working day.
  2. The screen shows this month's salary after cuts (days after the last day are cut), advance installments still due, and the deposit to return, with the final amount to pay or receive.
  3. Print or share the settlement letter for both signatures.
  4. The owner taps Finish settlement: remaining advances are cut now, the deposit is marked returned, and the employee is made inactive and logged out of their phone.

15. Security and privacy

  • PINs are never stored. Only a one-way hash is kept, mixed with a secret held by the server.
  • Wrong PINs: after 5 wrong tries the login is locked for 5 minutes, then 10, 20 and up to 60 minutes.
  • One phone = one employee, enforced by both the server and the app. Only the owner can reset it.
  • Fake GPS apps are detected and refused.
  • Setup code: the owner account can only be created once, with a secret code.
  • Audit log of every admin action, which can't be edited from the apps.
  • No third parties: data stays in your own Cloudflare account. The apps contain no ads, analytics or trackers.
  • Kiosk photos are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Money safety: managers' money and salary changes need the owner's approval, and paid months can be locked.
  • Check-in again after check-out needs approval and must be requested from inside the store.

16. Weak internet (offline punches)

  • If there's no internet when an employee taps Check In, the punch is saved on the phone and sent as soon as the internet is back.
  • The phone remembers the difference between its clock and the server's clock, so a wrong phone clock can't change the time. The server makes the final decision.
  • GPS is still checked. Punches synced later are marked offline so admin can see them, and a punch is never counted twice.
  • Alerts need the internet. When the phone is offline they arrive once it reconnects.

17. Setup and going live

  1. Cloudflare (free): create an account and an API token with the "Edit Cloudflare Workers" template.
  2. GitHub secrets: add CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN, CLOUDFLARE_ACCOUNT_ID, SETUP_CODE and PIN_PEPPER. Never change PIN_PEPPER later. For alerts, also GOOGLE_SERVICES_JSON and FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT (Firebase, free). The app signing key is locked by ANDROID_KEY_PASSWORD.
  3. Deploy: done only with the owner's OK, from the GitHub Actions tab ("Deploy backend").
  4. Install the admin app from the GitHub Release link. On first open, enter the setup code and choose your owner PIN.
  5. Settings: stand inside the store, tap "Use my current location", set the radius, and check the shifts, rules and holidays.
  6. Add employees with their salary, shift and a PIN, or use the bulk add.
  7. Install the employee app on each employee's phone and log in once. The phone is then locked to them. Allow location, camera and alerts when asked, and accept the store rules.
  8. Kiosk: on the counter phone, open the admin app and choose Kiosk mode.

18. Daily use, step by step

Employee

  1. Open the app and unlock with your fingerprint.
  2. At the store, tap Check In.
  3. For a break, tap Break, choose minutes and wait for approval. Tap Resume when back.
  4. Forgot something? Open Requests.
  5. Tap Check Out and confirm. Coming back later needs "Ask to check in again".

Manager

  1. Open the Live board in the morning.
  2. When the pending line appears, open Requests and approve or decline.
  3. Check in staff without a phone, with a reason.
  4. Check the late and absent lists at the end of the day.

Owner, month end

  1. Open Payroll and choose last month.
  2. Check each payslip and share it on WhatsApp.
  3. Mark each salary as paid.
  4. Lock the month.
  5. Every night, read the attendance summary alert.

New phone or lost phone

  1. Owner opens the employee and taps Reset phone.
  2. The employee logs in on the new phone.
  3. The old phone stops working at once.
